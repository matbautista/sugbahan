Our Story

Authentic Bacolod Flavor, Grilled to Perfection

Sugbahan means "grilling place" — and that's exactly our craft. Every piece of chicken is marinated in a signature blend of native vinegar, calamansi, and lemongrass, then slow-grilled over real charcoal for that unmistakable smoky char Bacolod is famous for. From our comforting Kansi and Sinigang to our grilled bangus, tilapia, and liempo, every dish is Filipino comfort food done the honest, authentic way.
